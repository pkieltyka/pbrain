title: Ledger cutover checklist
---

The ledger cutover happens on the last working day of the quarter. Freeze postings at noon, export the trial balance, and have a second person compare it with the bank statement before the new ledger opens.

If the comparison differs by any amount, the cutover is put off by one day.
