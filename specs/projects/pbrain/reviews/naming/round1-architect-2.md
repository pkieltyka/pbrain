Keep **`item.Item`**. An item is the unit an agent can cite by ID, verify, replace or retrieve. It may record a one-sentence decision, a learning, a note or a source. For a linked source, the item lives in the brain while the document it points to may live behind someone else’s access controls. “Open the item, then read the document at its source” tells a reader exactly what happens. “Document” blurs those two objects; “Note” already names one kind, and “Entry” brings no clearer meaning. A rename is cheaper before implementation, but it would still require coordinated edits to the spec, skill, command wording, types and component contracts. Keep the existing IDs and wire fields. A short Go doc comment defining Item will help more than a new name.
